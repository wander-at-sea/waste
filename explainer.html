Why "This" or "Next"?

On a weekday, the week works the way you'd expect: Monday to Sunday. Anything up to and including this coming Sunday gets called This; the seven days after that get called Next.

But ask on a Saturday or Sunday and something interesting happens to how people actually think about time: the weekend already feels like it belongs to the working week ahead, not the one just gone. So on a Saturday or Sunday, the cutoff for This slides forward to the coming Friday instead of Sunday — meaning the very next Monday still counts as This Monday, not Next Monday.

Either way, once you're past that second block of seven days, guessing gets silly — so it just tells you the plain day name with no label at all.
